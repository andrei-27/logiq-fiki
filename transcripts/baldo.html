Conducted in Romanian. Each turn shows the Romanian transcript followed by an English translation. Select a timestamp to play the recording from that point.

How this transcript was made

Status: machine-assisted, not yet checked against the audio by a human listener. The recording was transcribed on a local computer with two open speech-recognition models (Whisper large-v3 and Whisper large-v3-turbo). The audio was not uploaded to any online service. Where the two models disagreed, the more consistent reading was kept only if it also fit the surrounding sentence; otherwise the passage is marked unclear. Each unclear spot was then re-checked by transcribing a short clip around it three times: with each model, and once more with the preceding words given as context. A gap was filled only where both models agreed. Markers were removed where no pass found any words, which most likely means a pause, breath or filler. The English translation was written from the Romanian transcript and is not an authoritative translation.

  • [neclar] / [unclear] — speech that could not be recognised reliably, including short sounds that neither model transcribed.
  • word[?] — a probable but uncertain reading.
  • Speaker label with (?) — the speaker could not be determined confidently.
  • Hesitations and filler sounds are mostly omitted by the speech models; punctuation is approximate.
  • Timestamps mark the start of each turn (minutes:seconds).

Notes on specific passages
